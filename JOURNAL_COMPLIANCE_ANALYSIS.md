## 5. CITATIONS & REFERENCES

### 5.1 Citation Style: Author-Date (Harvard-like)

**In-text citations**:

| Authors | Format | Example |
|---------|--------|---------|
| 1 author | (Author, Year) | (Stoker, 1992) |
| 2 authors | (Author and Author, Year) | (Lyn and Goodwin, 1987) |
| 3+ authors | (First Author et al., Year) | (Xie et al., 2004) |
| Same author, same year | Add letter | (Zienkiewicz, 1995a, 1995b) |
| Multiple citations | Alphabetical, then chronological | (Allan, 2000a, 2000b; Allan and Jones, 1999) |

**Reference list format**:

```
Journal:
Author, A.B., Author, C.D., Year. Title of paper. Journal Name. Volume(Issue), Pages. https://doi.org/XXX.

Book:
Author, A.B., Year. Book Title, Edition ed. Publisher, Location.

Proceedings:
Author, A.B., Year. Title of paper. In: Proceedings of Conference Name. Publisher, Location, pp. XX-XX.
```

### 5.2 Reference List Requirements

- Alphabetical by surname
- Then chronological for same author
- **Include ALL authors** (not et al. in reference list)
- DOI required for journal articles
- Non-English works: add "(in Chinese)" etc.

### 5.3 Prohibited Sources

Not explicitly stated, but:
- Unpublished results: Not recommended (may mention in text only)
- Personal communications: Not recommended
- Preprints: Allowed if marked and central to work
